Flip the Frame

Candidate images for Deborah D. Douglas's chapter: storytellers in Chicago modeling asset-framing in the field.

About these photos

These photos depict Flip the Frame tours that model the asset-framing technique for storytellers. Featured are urban historian Shermann “Dilla” Thomas, Folded Map creator Tonika Johnson and Corey Luckett, owner of Englewood Branded, a clothing line and youth resource center in Chicago's Englewood neighborhood that dedicates 10% of sales to local youth engagement and community spaces. They are mentioned in my chapter.
